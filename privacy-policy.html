Privacy Policy for TRACE: Cyber Detective

Effective date: October 27, 2026

TRACE: Cyber Detective is developed and published by PKP Byte Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how TRACE: Cyber Detective handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

TRACE: Cyber Detective does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or payment information.

The app may store gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • Completed and unlocked cases
  • Scores, XP, rankings, achievements, and progress
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Player or investigator name entered within the app
  • Sound and other app preferences

This information is used only to provide game functionality and maintain your progress.

Local Storage

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

PKP Byte Studio does not operate a server for storing this gameplay information and does not use it to identify individual users.

Removing the app or clearing its local application data may permanently delete locally stored progress.

Device Permissions and Features

TRACE: Cyber Detective may use device capabilities required for supported app functionality.

The app does not use these capabilities to collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Advertising

The current version of TRACE: Cyber Detective does not contain third-party advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing and Sale

PKP Byte Studio does not sell users' personal information.

The current version of TRACE: Cyber Detective does not intentionally transmit or share users' locally stored gameplay information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

TRACE: Cyber Detective is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If the app's intended audience or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated as required.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because gameplay information is stored locally on the user's device, users can remove that information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

PKP Byte Studio does not maintain user accounts or a remote database containing users' gameplay information in the current version of the app.

Security

PKP Byte Studio aims to minimise data collection and uses local device storage where practical.

No method of digital storage or software operation can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the app is designed to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

Third-Party Services

TRACE: Cyber Detective is built using software libraries and development tools that support the operation of the application.

If a future version introduces third-party services that collect or process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to disclose those services and their purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TRACE: Cyber Detective's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
